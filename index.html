About

Research depth with an engineering mindset.

I earned an M.S. in Computer Science at Troy University with a concentration in Computer Networks & Security. My graduate research centers on reliable and deployable machine learning, especially self-supervised visual representations and hybrid CNN–Transformer systems under domain shift.

My broader research connects trustworthy AI with cybersecurity, AI governance, graph learning, anomaly detection, and data-intensive decision systems. I also bring industry experience in IT support, deployment automation, software development, and technical operations.
